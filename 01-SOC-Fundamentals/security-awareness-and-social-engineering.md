# Security Awareness and Social Engineering

## 1. What Is Security Awareness?

Security awareness is about educating employees so they understand the attacks and techniques used by attackers.

Employees who understand these techniques are better prepared to recognise suspicious activities and avoid falling for attackers' tricks.

This user education is also called **security awareness training**.

## 2. What Is Social Engineering?

Social engineering is a technique attackers use to trick people into performing actions that can compromise their organisation's systems.

Instead of relying only on technical weaknesses, attackers take advantage of human behaviour.

For example, attackers may trick employees into:

- Clicking on malicious links.
- Downloading malicious files.

When an employee clicks a malicious link or downloads and opens a harmful file, the system may become compromised.

## 3. Why Is Security Awareness Important?

An organisation may have several security tools and technologies, such as:

- Firewalls
- Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS)
- Access control
- Data Loss Prevention (DLP)
- Antivirus
- DNS security

However, having these tools does not guarantee protection if employees are unaware of social engineering techniques.

Attackers may exploit this lack of awareness to compromise systems.

Security awareness helps employees understand the tricks attackers use and make safer decisions.

## 4. Examples of Security Awareness Practices

Organisations, including banks and other service providers, educate users through security guidelines and warnings.

Examples include:

- Keeping computers free from malware.
- Changing passwords periodically.
- Never responding to communications requesting passwords.
- Never revealing passwords or card details to anyone.
- Being cautious about requests for sensitive information over phone calls or links.
- Understanding that banks will not ask customers to share their OTPs.

These practices help users recognise suspicious requests and protect sensitive information.

## 5. Security Awareness in a SOC

Security awareness is an important part of an organisation's overall security approach.

A SOC may have skilled personnel and sophisticated security tools, but employees who are unaware of common attack techniques can still expose the organisation to security risks.

Educating employees helps strengthen the organisation's security beyond its technical controls.

## 6. Key Takeaways

- Security awareness training focuses on educating employees about cyber threats and attacker techniques.
- Social engineering involves manipulating people into performing actions that may compromise systems.
- Attackers may trick employees into clicking malicious links or downloading harmful files.
- Security tools alone cannot guarantee protection if users are unaware of social engineering.
- User education is an important part of an organisation's security.
