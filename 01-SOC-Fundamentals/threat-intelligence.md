# Threat Intelligence

## 1. Introduction

Threat Intelligence is the process of collecting information about threats and attacks that may affect an organisation's systems, networks, and devices.

The collected information helps security teams understand potential threats and strengthen their security defences before attackers can cause damage.

Threat intelligence is an important part of proactive security.

## 2. Why Is Threat Intelligence Important?

Threat intelligence helps organisations:

- Understand emerging cyber threats and attack techniques.
- Learn about attacks targeting other organisations.
- Share relevant threat information with security teams.
- Strengthen existing security defences.
- Reduce the likelihood of similar attacks affecting their own systems.

## 3. How Does Threat Intelligence Work?

A basic threat intelligence process involves the following activities:

1. **Collect information:** Gather information about threats and attacks that may affect the organisation.
2. **Analyse the information:** Understand the threats and identify how they could affect the organisation.
3. **Share information:** Communicate relevant findings to the security team.
4. **Strengthen defences:** Use the information to improve security measures and reduce the risk of similar attacks.

## 4. Practical Example

Imagine that a security team learns about a new attack technique being used against several companies.

The team shares this information with the organisation's security personnel.

The security team then reviews its existing defences and applies appropriate security measures to reduce the possibility of the same attack affecting its systems.

This is how threat intelligence helps an organisation prepare for potential attacks.

## 5. Threat Intelligence in a SOC

Threat intelligence supports the proactive security activities of a Security Operations Centre (SOC).

SOC teams use threat information to understand potential attacks and improve their security defences.

For example, threat intelligence can help a SOC team identify attack techniques that may target the organisation and take preventive measures before damage occurs.

## 6. Threat Intelligence vs Threat Hunting

Threat intelligence and threat hunting are related, but they have different purposes.

| Threat Intelligence | Threat Hunting |
|---|---|
| Collects and analyses information about threats and attacks. | Searches for hidden threats within an organisation's environment. |
| Helps understand potential attacks and techniques. | Helps identify malicious activities that may have bypassed existing defences. |
| Supports stronger security defences. | Helps discover threats that may not have generated alerts. |

## 7. Key Takeaway

Threat intelligence helps an organisation understand potential cyber threats by collecting and sharing information about attacks and attacker techniques.

By using this information, security teams can strengthen their defences and reduce the likelihood of future attacks.

**Remember:** Threat intelligence helps organisations learn about threats so they can prepare and defend themselves.
